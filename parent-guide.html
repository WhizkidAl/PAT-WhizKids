My Base — complete parent guide

Open this guide in My Base → Family setup → Read the complete parent guide. Updated 16 November 2026. This guide is for adults; the child’s picture manual stays short and practical.

Start here

1. Open My Base. Use the child’s nominated tablet account on the tablet and your parent account on your own device.
2. Tap the snake to reach My day, My practice, Explore and Make something.
3. On your own device, sign in as a parent. Open Family connection to set or reset the Family PIN. The PIN protects family controls; it does not replace account sign-in.
4. Try one real activity together, record it once, approve it from your parent account and check Saved to cloud before closing.
5. Test Help together and confirm you receive the email. A “sent” message does not mean someone has read it.

The tablet unlock code, Family PIN and Google account password are different. The blank PIN space in the child’s manual is for the child’s tablet unlock code, written by the child. Keep the adult Family PIN private.

What the four choices do

My day: calendar links, appointments, reminders and daily routines. My practice: agreed activities, Therapy and school, feedback, weekly progress and coins. Explore: interest-based websites. Make something: creative tools and the optional My creations folder.

Therapy and school has Speech, OT, PBS and School. Each provider keeps its own resources up to date. A link may open a different service or app with its own sign-in. Return to the original My Base tab to give feedback.

Change links, pictures and appearance without coding

Open Family setup → Explore and Make choices. For each choice you can change its name, picture emoji and HTTPS link, or untick Show this choice. Choose a colour theme and standard or larger card text. Use Add a choice to add a website to Explore or Make something. Your added choices have a Remove button. Removing a shortcut does not delete the work in that service. Save with Save links and appearance, then check the cloud save status.

To change a provider’s destination, open Links, update the relevant Speech, OT, PBS or School address and choose Save setup. Use the family-accessible resource address supplied by that provider. Check the link on the child’s tablet too; a link that works in your signed-in account may not work in theirs.

Under Import links and character picture, choose an image to change the character picture. Under Morning and afternoon routines, enter one step per line as `emoji | task name`, then Save setup. Appearance controls change the colour theme and activity-card text size; they are not a free-form page designer. The four main navigation choices keep their familiar layout.

You can help your child learn to add a creation link together. Adding a shortcut does not create a practice requirement or coin entitlement. Agreed practice tasks, finish criteria, duration and weekly targets are managed by the clinician in consultation with the family. Your parent account cannot increase them.

Put an activity on hold or replace it

Open Family setup → Activity availability and icons. Choose Not ready to place a visible sign on the activity and stop it opening. Speech starts on hold while a suitable replacement is arranged. Other activities without a usable link also show Not ready.

For each activity you can enter a replacement HTTPS link, choose a simple icon, type an emoji or upload a PNG, JPG or WebP picture. Check the new link under the child’s account, choose Ready and tap Save availability and icons. Enter the Family PIN when asked and check the cloud save status. This changes the destination and picture only; the clinician still manages agreed tasks and coin targets.

My book displays the family’s printed welcome manual when it has been added to their private workspace. Use Back and Next to turn its pages. If it has not been added, the app says it is not ready.

Practice changes as the child’s needs change

Usual practice is 10–15 minutes, often about 15 minutes, with support and breaks as needed. Read the instructions for the activity currently assigned. Different activities have different finish points; a particular story or game is not a permanent reward rule. The clinician can replace tasks and update their instructions, frequency and finish criteria.

For ordinary practice, the parent approval screen asks for actual engaged minutes; it requires at least ten minutes. Do not count time the app was merely open or time spent on breaks. A specifically assigned task uses its clinician-set finish criterion, which can differ from the general duration. The approval screen shows that criterion. If the task or duration no longer fits, ask for it to be changed rather than marking unfinished work as finished.

Support, modelling, pointing, communication aids and asking for help are welcome. Coins recognise completion of the agreed work, not a perfect answer or an unsupported performance. If the child stops early, record that honestly. Opening and closing a page alone does not earn a coin.

Coins: the full rules

Ordinary practice: one approved completed session can earn one coin, up to the agreed target for that activity in that Monday–Sunday week. If Nessy has a target of two, the first two approved completions earn two coins altogether. A third or fourth session can be recorded but earns no further ordinary Nessy coin. An activity without an agreed target does not gain coins just because a shortcut has been added.

Approval: the child records feedback; it stays pending. Sign in with your parent account, open My coins → Dad or Mum checks, enter the Family PIN, record actual engaged minutes where asked and confirm the agreed work. The tablet account cannot award coins. Do not approve the same session twice or claim individual steps of an already claimed activity.

Finish-all bonus: once all positive weekly practice targets have been met through approved finished work, the app offers one completion bonus. It still needs parent approval. Manual corrections to a displayed total do not create coins or satisfy the bonus.

Extra-work bonus: after all the agreed weekly work is complete, the child must complete a separate additional session in an agreed practice area. Approve that actual session. From Sunday, the app can then offer one extra-work bonus for that week. It remains hidden until both conditions are met. Repeating work before completing the agreed plan does not qualify as this after-completion extra session. It does not matter how many extra sessions are done: there is at most one extra-work bonus per week.

Weekly maximum: add the ordinary practice targets, then at most two weekly bonuses. For targets of 2 + 1 + 1 + 1, the maximum is five ordinary practice coins plus two bonuses: seven. This example is not a permanent prescription. Whole daily routines are handled separately from the weekly practice plan: one completed routine claim per day, not one per step. Existing collected coins remain in the collection across weeks; a new week resets the opportunity to meet that week’s targets, not the saved collection.

Mistakes: Family setup → Remove individual coins lets you remove a mistaken coin with the Family PIN while keeping its activity record. Correct a weekly total records a correction and reason without creating coins. Ask the clinician about plan changes; do not use corrections to manufacture bonus eligibility.

Simple wording for the child

I earn one coin each time I finish an agreed activity, up to my weekly target. Dad checks my work. When I reach the target, I can still practise without another activity coin.

Finish all my weekly targets: one bonus coin. Do extra practice after that: one more bonus coin at the end of the week. Dad checks both.

Save creations to the tablet or Google Drive

My Base opens creative tools; it does not automatically save their projects. In the tool used, choose its Save, Export or Download option. Keep an editable project if you want to work on it again; an exported picture or video may not be editable in the same way.

With the parent supervising, check that Google Drive is signed into the child’s account. Create a private My creations folder. Upload a small test creation, reopen it to check it saved, and agree which items should be kept on the tablet or in Drive. In Family setup → Links, paste that folder’s HTTPS address into My creations and Save setup. The shortcut then appears under Make something. The child can learn these steps alongside the parent rather than having every task done for them.

Keep the folder private. Sharing the folder and adding its link to My Base are separate actions. Grant named people only the access they need; avoid anyone-with-the-link sharing for personal work. Check storage in the account and tablet settings before accumulating large videos. Review unwanted copies together; do not delete the only copy of a project. Do not buy more storage automatically.

Calendar, reminders and invitations

The calendar and Drive belong to the Google account used for them. They are separate from the My Base sign-in. Check the account name before editing or sharing. The parent approves any access to the child’s Google Calendar or Drive; this guide or an invitation does not grant access.

On a computer, use Google Calendar → Settings and sharing for the relevant calendar. If the supervised account allows sharing, invite only the agreed named account with the agreed permission. Do not make the calendar public. If sharing is unavailable, keep parent-managed entries and discuss an alternative; do not remove supervision to bypass it. Drive sharing must be arranged separately.

Use the same symbols in calendar titles and invitations:

  • 💼 In-person appointment: provider, date, start/end time, timezone, address and what to bring.
  • ☁️ Online appointment: provider, date, start/end time, timezone, joining link and adult support.
  • 🔔 Practice reminder: an opportunity to choose an agreed activity, not a booked appointment or evidence of practice.

The practice plan and calendar reminder times are separate. Changing a target in My Base does not edit Google Calendar. Multiple reminder windows are choices, not an obligation to practise at every reminder. Check the notification settings on the tablet itself.

Invite the school and other professionals — the parent sends

All invitations and requests to the school, teacher or other team members come from the parent’s own email account, signed by the parent. The clinician can help prepare wording and explain the program, but does not send these invitations as the parent. Review the recipient and wording before sending. Nothing in My Base sends team invitations automatically.

Explain that My Base is a Portable Access to Therapy (PAT) hub: a practical way for the child to find agreed resources, practise manageable tasks, request help and reflect. Participation is optional. Ask for one starting resource, clear instructions, adult support needed, a finish point, suggested frequency and review date. The family and clinician agree the overall workload before it becomes an active task.

Team members may suggest tasks, update their own resources and advise on progress. They do not gain account access through a letter, change coin rules or approve rewards. A parent must approve any named access request. The current viewing role may show shared activity records across the child’s program, so do not promise a separate discipline-only record. Formal reports remain in the provider’s approved record system.

For school Canva access, ask whether the child can join the school’s Canva Education class under its normal eligibility, account and consent arrangements. Eligible primary/secondary educators and their students can use Canva Education free; this does not mean every allied-health professional is eligible or that all other linked services are free. Confirm the account to invite before sending. See Canva Education.

Include the clinician’s agreed support contact in the body of your invitation, clearly labelled as the program contact. The parent remains the sender and the contact for consent. The personalised teacher and professional drafts are kept with the family’s parent resources; copy them into the parent’s email after checking them.

Help and talk-to-text

Help → choose a phrase → optionally Talk my message or type → check the words → Send to Dad. Selecting a phrase alone does not send an email. If speech input is unavailable, use the keyboard microphone or type. Permission and connection requirements vary by device.

The message goes to the configured parent recipient, not the teacher or therapist. It includes the selected request and activity and any extra written message. Check the actual destination during setup. The child cannot choose an arbitrary recipient. Test delivery together, including Junk. If sending fails or help is needed now, ask an adult nearby. Email is not an urgent-response service.

Voices and recordings

The system voice works without making a recording. Where an activity offers Our character voices, choose the story part, tap Record, allow microphone access if agreed, speak and Stop. Listen before saving. Use Save a copy to tablet to retain a separate copy. Recording starts only after Record is tapped.

Story Safari recordings stay in that tool’s browser storage; they are not automatically included in the My Base cloud record or its full backup. Clearing that browser’s data can remove them. My Base’s own recordings are separate. On a shared browser, other users may be able to play locally saved voices. Do not promise that all recordings travel between devices.

Tablet setup and website access

Use the current PAT address above. In Chrome, use the menu → Install app / Add to Home screen if needed. Keep a working shortcut until the replacement opens the correct account and saved work. Do not uninstall or clear browser data just to refresh an icon. To add a calendar widget, hold an empty Home-screen area → Widgets → Calendar, then verify the displayed account.

Review each selected website/app through the tablet’s parental controls. A new hostname or sign-in page may need separate parent approval. Approve specific services rather than unrestricted browsing. An app-store link does not install an app; the parent approves installation and any cost. My Base checks for updates when it returns to the foreground, reconnects, and while it is open. It applies them at Home once work is safely saved; an activity, recording or unsaved work delays the update. Return Home with an internet connection and allow the save to finish. The tablet’s shortcut picture may refresh separately.

Saving, backups, reports and problems

My Base saves its records and settings to the private PAT workspace. Check the saved/pending status. If changes are waiting, reconnect and keep browser data intact. A Full backup contains personal links, settings, records and My Base recordings: keep it privately. Before moving devices, save a backup and verify the cloud save. A screenshot or preview is not a backup.

To share progress, use This week → choose the week → Download this week’s report. Review it and send it yourself to agreed recipients. Reports are not automatically emailed to providers. Full backups are not routine team reports. Outside tools have their own saving and backup arrangements.

If a link fails, check the URL, signed-in account and parental controls. If a change fails to save, retain the open app, check connectivity and note the exact message. If approval is unavailable, confirm you are signed into the parent account, not the child account. Ask the program contact for help with access or agreed-task changes. Never send passwords, PINs or complete backups in a public issue report.

Parent handover check

  • Parent sign-in and Family PIN work on the parent’s device.
  • The tablet uses the nominated child account and current PAT address.
  • The child can find all four choices and return Home.
  • Current practice instructions and weekly targets are understood.
  • All selected links work under the child’s account and parental controls.
  • A real completed session saves, is approved correctly and remains after reopening.
  • Help email arrives at the correct parent inbox.
  • A real creation has been saved and reopened successfully.
  • Calendar account, sharing decision, symbols and notifications are checked.
  • Parent-authored invitations are reviewed before sending; access is granted separately.
  • Backup and recording locations are understood.
