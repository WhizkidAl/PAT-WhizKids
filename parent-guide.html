WHIZ KIDS · PORTABLE ACCESS TO THERAPY

Welcome to PAT Base

PAT means Portable Access to Therapy. PAT Base brings your child’s routines, agreed activities and creative tools together. We’re still trying it out together: please tell Alex what works, what is confusing and what needs changing.

1. Connect to Wi-Fi

On the tablet, open Settings → Connections → Wi-Fi. Choose your network and enter its password.

2. Open PAT Base

Tap the snake shortcut (it may still say My Base). If sign-in is needed, use the tablet account arranged with Alex. For your parent access, open PAT Base on your own phone or computer, enter your nominated parent email and choose Email me a sign-in link. Open the newest link on that same device. If no workspace appears, contact Alex.

3. Set your Family PIN

While signed in to your parent account, open Family connection → Set or reset the Family PIN. Choose 4–8 digits, enter them twice and tap Save Family PIN. Set it once and reuse it for adult controls and approvals. Keep it private: it is not the child’s tablet unlock code.

4. Find the guides

Open Family setup → Read the complete parent guide. It has short sections for setup, updates, activities and accounts. My book opens the child’s picture manual when supplied. The parent guide also contains a ready-to-copy teacher email for Canva Education.

5. Have a look around

Start with what is already on the device. Some choices need a separate account, school invitation or parent approval. Not ready means that choice is on hold. There is no need to set everything up today.

Keeping it up to date

To pick up an update: connect to Wi-Fi, open PAT Base and tap Home inside the app. Finish the activity and let saving complete. Updates apply automatically when it is safe. If the screen is still old, close PAT Base from Recent apps and reopen it. Do not uninstall it or clear its data.

What the four choices do

My day: calendar links, appointments, reminders and daily routines. My practice: agreed activities, Therapy and school, feedback, weekly progress and coins. Explore: interest-based websites. Make something: creative tools and the optional My creations folder.

Therapy and school has Speech, OT, PBS and School. Each provider keeps its own resources up to date. A link may open a different service or app with its own sign-in. Return to the original PAT Base tab to give feedback.

Change links, pictures and appearance without coding

Sign in with the parent account → Family setup. In Explore and Make choices, change names, links, emojis and visibility, or Add a choice. In Activity availability and icons, replace a practice link and picture, change the names of the three practice-app buttons, or mark a tool Not ready. Save each panel and check Saved to cloud.

Choose B · Charcoal and electric purple, Purple and white, Ocean and ice, Forest and sage, or Plum and rose. Change card text size if needed. The start-screen character can be replaced with a PNG/JPG/WebP under 1 MB. The tablet shortcut retains the shared PAT snake icon.

The four main sections, their purpose and Whiz Kids branding stay fixed. Hiding a button does not uninstall or block an outside app; use Family Link for device restrictions. Weekly practice and coin settings are in a separate panel.

Put an activity on hold or replace it

Open Family setup → Activity availability and icons. Choose Not ready to place a visible sign on the activity and stop it opening. Speech starts on hold while a suitable replacement is arranged. Other activities without a usable link also show Not ready.

For each activity you can enter a replacement HTTPS link, choose a simple icon, type an emoji or upload a PNG, JPG or WebP picture. Check the new link under the child’s account, choose Ready and tap Save availability and icons. Enter the Family PIN when asked and check the cloud save status. This changes the destination and picture only; the clinician still manages agreed tasks and coin targets.

My book displays the family’s printed welcome manual when it has been added to their private workspace. Use Back and Next to turn its pages. If it has not been added, the app says it is not ready.

Practice changes as needs change

Providers recommend the current task, finish point, support and minutes/sessions. The parent adds or changes it in Family setup → Assign a specific activity. A positive weekly frequency marks a task as agreed. Set the overall allowance in Weekly practice plan; adding a resource does not automatically raise it.

Parents can change links, availability, tasks and the plan using their parent account and PIN. Changes to a week already containing practice start the following Monday, keeping recorded rewards intact.

Coins: the full rules

One coin per full 15 minutes of agreed, parent-checked practice. Choose session minutes and sessions per week in Family setup → Weekly practice plan. Three 30-minute sessions suggests six coins. You can set a different whole weekly allowance. Save to activate; the previous plan remains until then.

  1. The child opens an agreed activity, does the work and tells how it went. Opening a website or leaving it running earns nothing.
  2. The parent signs in, checks the current task and records actual practice minutes, excluding breaks. 15 minutes = one coin; 30 = two. A 20-minute session earns one; fewer than 15 earns none but stays recorded. Minutes do not carry between sessions.
  3. Ordinary coins stop at the shared weekly allowance. Duplicate session claims do not earn again. Routine ticks and Explore do not add separate practice coins.
  4. From Sunday, completing both the planned sessions and weekly practice allowance unlocks one completion bonus request.
  5. A separate agreed session of at least 15 minutes started after that completed plan can unlock one extra-work bonus request. Both bonuses need parent approval, once each per week.

A five-coin plan therefore allows up to seven coins; a six-coin plan up to eight. Extra practice does not create unlimited coins. Once work is recorded, a changed plan starts next Monday. Earlier coins stay under their original rules. The timer does not prove engagement; approval is the adult’s check.

Save creations to the tablet or Google Drive

PAT Base opens creative tools; it does not automatically save their projects. In the tool used, choose its Save, Export or Download option. Keep an editable project if you want to work on it again; an exported picture or video may not be editable in the same way.

With the parent supervising, check that Google Drive is signed into the child’s account. Create a private My creations folder. Upload a small test creation, reopen it to check it saved, and agree which items should be kept on the tablet or in Drive. In Family setup → Links, paste that folder’s HTTPS address into My creations and Save setup. The shortcut then appears under Make something. The child can learn these steps alongside the parent rather than having every task done for them.

Keep the folder private. Sharing the folder and adding its link to PAT Base are separate actions. Grant named people only the access they need; avoid anyone-with-the-link sharing for personal work. Check storage in the account and tablet settings before accumulating large videos. Review unwanted copies together; do not delete the only copy of a project. Do not buy more storage automatically.

Calendar, reminders and invitations

The calendar and Drive belong to the Google account used for them. They are separate from the PAT Base sign-in. Check the account name before editing or sharing. The parent approves any access to the child’s Google Calendar or Drive; this guide or an invitation does not grant access.

On a computer, use Google Calendar → Settings and sharing for the relevant calendar. If the supervised account allows sharing, invite only the agreed named account with the agreed permission. Do not make the calendar public. If sharing is unavailable, keep parent-managed entries and discuss an alternative; do not remove supervision to bypass it. Drive sharing must be arranged separately.

Use the same symbols in calendar titles and invitations:

  • 💼 In-person appointment: provider, date, start/end time, timezone, address and what to bring.
  • ☁️ Online appointment: provider, date, start/end time, timezone, joining link and adult support.
  • 🔔 Practice reminder: an opportunity to choose an agreed activity, not a booked appointment or evidence of practice.

The practice plan and calendar reminder times are separate. Changing a target in PAT Base does not edit Google Calendar. Multiple reminder windows are choices, not an obligation to practise at every reminder. Check the notification settings on the tablet itself.

Invite providers — send from your own email

Open Team invitation and resource pack, fill in the family details and copy the email into your own email account. It asks providers to supply one maintained link and a clear practice recommendation, and sets out the appointment format. Routine onboarding does not need to come back to Alexandra.

Add the child’s Google address as an attendee on appointments as well as the parent address. Email CC alone is not enough. Sharing the pack does not grant access to private records or accounts.

Help and talk-to-text

Help → choose a phrase → optionally Talk my message or type → check the words → Send to Dad. Selecting a phrase alone does not send an email. If speech input is unavailable, use the keyboard microphone or type. Permission and connection requirements vary by device.

The message goes to the configured parent recipient, not the teacher or therapist. It includes the selected request and activity and any extra written message. Check the actual destination during setup. The child cannot choose an arbitrary recipient. Test delivery together, including Junk. If sending fails or help is needed now, ask an adult nearby. Email is not an urgent-response service.

Voices and recordings

The system voice works without making a recording. Where an activity offers Our character voices, choose the story part, tap Record, allow microphone access if agreed, speak and Stop. Listen before saving. Use Save a copy to tablet to retain a separate copy. Recording starts only after Record is tapped.

Story Safari recordings stay in that tool’s browser storage; they are not automatically included in the PAT Base cloud record or its full backup. Clearing that browser’s data can remove them. PAT Base’s own recordings are separate. On a shared browser, other users may be able to play locally saved voices. Do not promise that all recordings travel between devices.

Tablet setup and website access

Use the current PAT address above. In Chrome, use the menu → Install app / Add to Home screen if needed. Keep a working shortcut until the replacement opens the correct account and saved work. Do not uninstall or clear browser data just to refresh an icon. To add a calendar widget, hold an empty Home-screen area → Widgets → Calendar, then verify the displayed account.

Review each selected website/app through the tablet’s parental controls. A new hostname or sign-in page may need separate parent approval. Approve specific services rather than unrestricted browsing. An app-store link does not install an app; the parent approves installation and any cost. PAT Base checks for updates when it returns to the foreground, reconnects, and while it is open. It applies them at Home once work is safely saved; an activity, recording or unsaved work delays the update. Return Home with an internet connection and allow the save to finish. The tablet’s shortcut picture may refresh separately.

Saving, backups, reports and problems

PAT Base saves its records and settings to the private PAT workspace. Check the saved/pending status. If changes are waiting, reconnect and keep browser data intact. A Full backup contains personal links, settings, records and PAT Base recordings: keep it privately. Before moving devices, save a backup and verify the cloud save. A screenshot or preview is not a backup.

To share progress, use This week → choose the week → Download this week’s report. Review it and send it yourself to agreed recipients. Reports are not automatically emailed to providers. Full backups are not routine team reports. Outside tools have their own saving and backup arrangements.

If a link fails, check the URL, signed-in account and parental controls. If a change fails to save, retain the open app, check connectivity and note the exact message. If approval is unavailable, confirm you are signed into the parent account, not the child account. Ask the program contact for help with access or agreed-task changes. Never send passwords, PINs or complete backups in a public issue report.

Parent handover check
  • Parent sign-in and Family PIN work on the parent’s device.
  • The tablet uses the nominated child account and current PAT address.
  • The child can find all four choices and return Home.
  • Current practice instructions and weekly targets are understood.
  • All selected links work under the child’s account and parental controls.
  • A real completed session saves, is approved correctly and remains after reopening.
  • Help email arrives at the correct parent inbox.
  • A real creation has been saved and reopened successfully.
  • Calendar account, sharing decision, symbols and notifications are checked.
  • Parent-authored invitations are reviewed before sending; access is granted separately.
  • Backup and recording locations are understood.
Telehealth: Teams, Meet and Zoom

Prepare only the services your team uses. On the tablet, open Google Play and install the official Microsoft Teams, Google Meet and/or Zoom Workplace app. A link here does not install it remotely.

  1. Dad approves the chosen app and any required sign-in under the child’s supervised account.
  2. On the tablet: Settings → Apps → the chosen app → Permissions → allow Camera and Microphone while using the app. Family Link may require Dad to approve these permissions.
  3. Open a test calendar invitation and tap the direct Join appointment link. Choose the matching app, enter the child’s display name and wait for the provider to admit them. Some organisations require a signed-in account or verification; ask the provider beforehand.
  4. Test sound and video with an adult before the first appointment. Help the child leave the meeting afterwards and return to PAT Base.

Limit or restore access: on Dad’s phone, open Family Link → select the child → Screen time / Time limits → App limits → choose the app → Block, set a limit or allow it again. Labels may vary. Camera/microphone permission can also be changed in the tablet’s App permissions or Family Link → child’s device → App permissions. Keep Chrome available for PAT Base. Website approvals and app approvals are separate.

Removing a button in PAT Base only hides the link. To restrict the installed app itself, use Family Link. Recheck a real invitation after any restriction change.
